Overview

Cohen's κ corrects raw agreement for chance (1 = perfect, 0 = no better than chance, <0 = worse). Rough guide: <0.2 poor, 0.2–0.4 fair, 0.4–0.6 moderate, 0.6–0.8 substantial, >0.8 almost perfect. Human majority = most common human label; ties are excluded. "Positive" = jailbreak.
